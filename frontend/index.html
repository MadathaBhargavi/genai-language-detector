How the GenAI Language Detector Works

01

Burstiness & Sentence Cadence

Human writers naturally alternate between rapid-fire short sentences and complex multi-clause sentences. In contrast, large language models maintain an unusually uniform, consistent sentence length.

02

Perplexity & Predictability

Language models select high-probability token transitions, yielding lower statistical entropy. Natural human prose features idiosyncratic phrasing, personal quirks, and spontaneous vocabulary choice.

03

Discourse & Syntactic Markers

Analyzes over-indexed transitional phrases ("crucial", "delve", "testament", "tapestry"), contraction frequencies, and punctuation variance to calibrate an overall posterior probability.
